## 3. Elicitation Session Guide

### 3.1 Session Objectives
Gather comprehensive business requirements for ERP implementation including finance, HR, supply chain, and customer management modules.

### 3.2 Key Questions

#### Opening (Warm-up)
What are the current pain points in your department?
What success looks like for this initiative?

#### Discovery
What features are essential for daily operations?
What integrations are required with existing systems?
What reports and dashboards are needed?

#### Constraints & Risks
What budget or timeline constraints exist?
What technical limitations should we be aware of?

#### Wrap-up
