## Executive Summary

**Project Name:** Enterprise ERP Implementation
**Date:** 2026-05-29
**Prepared By:** Project Management Office

### Problem Statement
Current legacy systems are unable to support business growth and digital transformation initiatives. Manual processes create operational inefficiencies and data silos across departments.

### Recommended Solution
Implement integrated cloud-based ERP system to unify business processes, enable real-time reporting, and support scalable growth.

### Expected Benefits
Improved operational efficiency (30%), reduced manual work (50%), better data visibility, and enhanced customer satisfaction.
