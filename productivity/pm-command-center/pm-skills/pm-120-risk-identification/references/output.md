## RAG Rating Guide
- **Red**: Score 18-25 (Critical - immediate action required)
- **Amber**: Score 10-17 (High - action plan needed)
- **Green**: Score 1-9 (Low - monitor and review)

## Risk Score Calculation
`Score = Probability (1-5) × Impact (1-5)`

## Instructions
1. Identify risks during planning and execution phases
2. Update status regularly (weekly minimum)
3. Escalate Red-rated risks immediately to sponsor
4. Review and close risks when no longer applicable
