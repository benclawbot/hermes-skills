## Priority Levels
- **Critical**: Blocks progress, needs immediate resolution (< 24 hours)
- **High**: Significant impact, resolution needed this week
- **Medium**: Moderate impact, can be addressed in sprint
- **Low**: Minor impact, address when possible

## Status Definitions
- **Open**: Identified, not yet started
- **In Progress**: Being worked on
- **Waiting**: Blocked on external dependency
- **Resolved**: Solution implemented, pending verification
- **Closed**: Verified complete and documented

## Weekly Review Checklist
- [ ] Review all open issues
- [ ] Update status and resolution notes
- [ ] Escalate stalled issues
- [ ] Confirm closure of resolved items
- [ ] Identify new issues from week

## Escalation Path
1. Assignee → PM (if blocked > 2 days)
2. PM → Sponsor (if blocked > 1 week)
3. Sponsor → Steering Committee (if critical)
