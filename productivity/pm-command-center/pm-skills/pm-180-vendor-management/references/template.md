## Quality Rating Scale
- **5 - Exceptional**: Exceeds expectations
- **4 - Good**: Meets all requirements
- **3 - Acceptable**: Meets basic requirements
- **2 - Below Standard**: Missing some requirements
- **1 - Poor**: Does not meet requirements

## Performance Rating Scale
- **5 - Excellent**: Always on time, proactive
- **4 - Good**: On time, responsive
- **3 - Acceptable**: Sometimes late, adequate
- **2 - Concerning**: Often late, slow response
- **1 - Unacceptable**: Misses deadlines, non-responsive

## Payment Status
- ☐ Pending - Not yet due
- ☐ Invoiced - Awaiting payment
- ☐ Partial - Partially paid
- ☐ Paid - Complete
- ☐ Disputed - Under discussion
